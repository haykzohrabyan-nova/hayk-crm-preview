What Is BazaarPrinting CRM?
A full internal CRM built for a B2B printing company

Purpose

BazaarPrinting CRM manages the full customer journey — from the first contact (lead) through quote generation, customer approval, production, and payment. Every step is tracked, every action is logged, and the right people see the right information at the right time.

📥 Lead Management

SDRs capture and validate incoming leads. They screen, hold, or route leads to the Sales team. All activity is tracked in a full timeline.

📝 Quotes & Orders

Sales or SDRs create quotes with line items, pricing, discounts, and delivery details. Quotes are sent to customers via Email, SMS, or WhatsApp.

✅ Customer Approval

Each quote has a private link. The customer clicks, reviews the quote, and confirms — converting it into an order automatically with an ORD-YYYY-NNN reference code.

💳 Payment Tracking

Orders track payment status (Unpaid / Partial / Paid) and deposit status. Payment reminder links can be sent via Email, SMS, or WhatsApp directly from the order page.

👥 CRM & Customers

Every customer is saved to the CRM. Quotes auto-create customer profiles. Duplicate customer detection runs in real time when entering phone numbers.

📊 Role-Based Access

SDRs, Sales reps, and Admins each see a different view. Actions are gated by role. Admins have full override capability on any record.

User Roles
Three system roles — each with a dedicated workflow
SDR
Sales Development Rep
  • View and validate incoming leads
  • Hold, reject, or route leads to Sales
  • Create quotes for leads under HV threshold
  • Send quotes via Email/SMS/WhatsApp
  • See their own "Won" leads
  • Send payment reminders to customers
Sales Rep
Sales Pipeline
  • Claim routed leads from SDR
  • Work leads: hold, reject, or convert
  • Create and manage quotes & orders
  • Handle high-value quotes (no block)
  • Convert quotes to orders manually
  • Send payment reminders to customers
Admin
Administrator
  • Full access to all leads, quotes, orders
  • Override any locked record
  • Cancel or edit confirmed orders
  • Manage users, roles, permissions
  • Configure dropdowns, products, company info
  • Test and manage integrations
ℹ️
Admins can create custom roles with fine-grained page-level access control. Every page in the system is listed in the database, and role permissions are toggled through the Admin panel — no code changes required.

📱 Duplicate Detection

When an SDR types a phone number, the system instantly searches for matching customer profiles. If a match is found, the SDR can select the existing customer — preventing duplicate records in the CRM.

2+ matches List shown — pick one or create fresh

Won Tracking
How the system tracks successful conversions for SDRs and Sales
🏆
A lead is marked "Won" automatically — not manually — when the linked quote becomes a confirmed order. This happens whether the customer approves via the public link, or a rep clicks "Convert to Order".
🔍 SDR Won Tab

SDRs have a "Won" tab in their workspace showing all their leads that converted to orders. Each row shows:

  • Order reference (ORD-YYYY-NNN)
  • Total value ($)
  • Closer's name (who closed the deal)
  • Customer info
💼 Sales Won

Sales reps see "Won" in their pipeline stats. The lead's sales_status is auto-updated to "Won" when:

  • Customer clicks "Confirm & Accept" on the public quote page
  • Rep clicks "Convert to Order" in the CRM

Creating a Quote
Where quotes can be started and what they contain
📌 3 Entry Points
From a Lead
SDR/Sales clicks "New Quote" from within a lead. Customer info pre-filled. Customer tab hidden.
From CRM
"Add Quote" button on a customer profile. Customer pre-filled and locked.
New Quote button
Blank form. Rep enters customer details. Duplicate detection runs on phone entry.
📋 Quote Contents
  • Title, priority, due date, rush flag
  • Line items: product type, material, size, qty, unit price
  • Add-on finishings (UV, foil, perforation, die cut)
  • Shipping + tax rate + discount (% or $)
  • Payment methods
  • Prepayment: Full or Partial (% / fixed amount)
  • Send via: Email / SMS / WhatsApp / In-person
  • Follow-up schedule
  • Special requirements & internal notes
💰 Two Order Flows
Quote First

Send a quote for the customer to review and approve online. They get a private link, review everything, and click "Confirm & Accept." This auto-generates the order.

Direct Order

Customer is already committed. Skip the quote step — send a payment link directly. Good for walk-in customers or repeat clients.

📧 Email

Full HTML email with company branding, all line items, pricing summary, and a gold "View & Confirm Quote" button. Sent via Instantly AI.

💬 SMS

Short text message with the order total and a direct link to the quote page. Sent via Twilio. Phone numbers auto-normalised to international format.

📱 WhatsApp

Same message as SMS, delivered to the customer's WhatsApp number. Sent via Twilio WhatsApp channel.

🤝 In-Person

No message is sent. Status changes to "Sent" so the rep knows the quote has been presented. The public link is still available for the customer to sign off on.

🔗
Every quote has a unique, unguessable private URL (/q/[token]). No login is required for the customer. The link can be resent at any time from the quote detail page.

Record Locking
Protecting confirmed orders from accidental changes
🔒
Once a customer confirms a quote, the record is locked for all SDR and Sales users. They can view it and send payment reminders, but they cannot edit the line items, pricing, or cancel the order. Only an Admin can make changes or cancel a confirmed order.
📋 Lock States
SituationSDR / SalesAdmin
Draft or Sent quote ✅ Can edit & cancel ✅ Full access
Customer-confirmed order 🔒 View only + send payment reminders ✅ Full access (edit & cancel)
Manually converted order (rep) 🔒 View only + send payment reminders ✅ Full access
Cancelled ticket 🔒 View only 🔒 View only
✅
Payment reminders are always available regardless of lock state. A locked order can still have its payment link sent via Email, SMS, or WhatsApp directly from the order page — this is a separate action from editing.
🔔
Activity Log
Full audit trail of everything that happens in the system
📜
Every action in the CRM is logged automatically. The Activity Log page (/notifications) shows a paginated feed of all events across all users, leads, and tickets. Each entry shows who did what, when, and on which record.
📋 Lead Activity Events
  • Lead created / edited
  • Lead validated / status changed
  • Lead routed to Sales
  • Lead put on hold / resumed
  • Lead rejected (with reason)
  • Lead merged (duplicate resolution)
  • Sales rep claimed a lead
📋 Ticket/Order Activity Events
  • Ticket created / updated
  • Quote sent (or resent)
  • Customer confirmed the quote
  • Rep converted quote to order
  • Payment reminder sent (channel + destination logged)
  • Status changed (routed, claimed, cancelled)
⚡ Real-Time Updates

The system uses Supabase Realtime. When any lead or ticket changes — from any user, in any browser — everyone sees the update instantly without refreshing. Tab badge counts update live. The Activity Log refreshes automatically.

Integrations
External services connected to the CRM
📧 Instantly AI Live ✓

Sends all quote emails and payment reminder emails. Full HTML templates with company branding, line items, pricing, and CTA buttons. Configured in Admin → Integrations.

📱 Twilio Live ✓

Sends all SMS and WhatsApp messages. Toll-free number. Phone numbers are automatically formatted to international E.164 standard (+1XXXXXXXXXX). Configured in Admin → Integrations.

💳 Stripe Planned

Will handle online payment collection via the public quote page. Prepayment webhooks will auto-update deposit status. Placeholder is built; wiring is deferred.

🏦 Zelle Planned

Will match Zelle transfers via memo code. Manual "Mark as Paid" fallback available. Placeholder is built; wiring is deferred.

📄 PDF Export

Every quote and order can be downloaded as a PDF directly from the detail page. The PDF includes company letterhead, Bill To block, line items table, full pricing breakdown, payment methods, and a gold footer. One click — no print dialog.

⚙️
Admin Panel
Full control over users, settings, and the CRM configuration
👤 Users

Create users with a temporary password (no email needed). Assign roles. Deactivate accounts. Reset passwords. Require password change on next login.

🔑 Roles & Permissions

Create custom roles. Toggle page-level access for every role using a permissions matrix. All changes take effect immediately — no code changes required.

📋 Dropdown Options

All dropdown options (lead sources, industries, payment types, quote channels, product priorities, etc.) are managed here. Add, rename, reorder, or deactivate options.

🖨️ Products Catalog

Manage product types and materials available in the Line Items tab of quotes. Link materials to product types. Deactivate products without deleting historical data.

🏢 Company Settings

Logo, company name, address, phone, email, website. Default tax rate. High-Value Threshold amount. Rush surcharge %. All used in PDF exports and email templates.

🔗 Integrations

Configure and test Twilio (SMS/WhatsApp) and Instantly AI (email) directly from the admin panel. Send a test message to verify everything is working.

🔐
All users must log in with email + password, then complete a TOTP 2-Factor Authentication step (authenticator app). New users must change their temporary password on first login before accessing any page.
